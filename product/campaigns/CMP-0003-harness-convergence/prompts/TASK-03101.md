# TASK-03101 Declare the Owner-effect outcome and state when serialized admission stays in force

Role: Architect. Round R-0310, wave CTG-03101. Records: ADR-CHK-0003, ADR-CHK-0004.
Tier: architect (resolve the host model from `models.tiers` in campaign.json). Effort: medium. Time budget: 60 minutes.

## Steps

1. Read ADR-CHK-0003 (round close) and ADR-CHK-0004 (serialized admission), then
   law/schemas/campaign.schema.json and law/policy/campaign-execution.json; read the
   OE-01 entry and decision 5 of the campaign guide to see the fallback that must be
   recordable.
2. Add an optional `outcome` property to the Owner-effect items of the campaign schema, a
   closed enum of `performed` and `fallback`; keep additionalProperties false and every
   other field unchanged. State on the property that `fallback` means the effect was
   resolved by the fallback its description names and that performed_at then records
   when the fallback was recorded.
3. In law/policy/campaign-execution.json, amend isolation.serialized_admission so it is in
   force whenever the merge-queue effect has no performed_at or its outcome is fallback,
   and amend the round_close rule so a required effect closes its round when it carries
   performed_at with either outcome.
4. Rebuild, run release:bootstrap, and run the acceptance commands.

## Deliverables

- The campaign schema admits an optional outcome on every Owner effect, a closed enum of performed and fallback; an effect whose outcome is fallback records in performed_at the time the fallback was recorded, and every other field is unchanged (ADR-CHK-0003 round close).
- The campaign execution policy states that serialized admission is in force whenever the merge-queue Owner effect has no performed_at or its outcome is fallback, and that a round closes when each required effect carries performed_at with either outcome (ADR-CHK-0004 IA-005).
