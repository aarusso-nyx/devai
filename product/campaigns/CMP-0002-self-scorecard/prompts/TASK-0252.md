# TASK-0252 Classify security, performance, and robustness tests so the coverage sensors count them

Role: Engineer. Round R-0205, wave CTG-0252. Record: ADR-SCR-0004.
Tier: worker (resolve the host model from `models.tiers` in campaign.json). Effort: medium. Time budget: 120 minutes.

## Steps

1. Read the three sensors in `packages/sensors/src/` and their thresholds (security 5
   percent, robustness 10 percent). Decide one classification convention and state it in
   `docs/dev/testing.md`.
2. Apply the convention to existing tests under `packages/cli/tests/`,
   `packages/authority/tests/`, `packages/loop/tests/`, `tests/contract/`, and
   `tests/e2e/` by renaming or wrapping, without changing assertions.
3. Only if the sensors cannot express the convention, adjust their pattern matching, and
   keep thresholds unchanged.

## Deliverables

- The three coverage sensors document the classification they count (file name segment
  or describe prefix) and DEVAI's existing authority, forbidden-action, and error-
  contract tests carry it.
- test_security_coverage, test_performance_coverage, and test_robustness_coverage report
  pass in the sweep against the merged head.
- The testing guide states the classification convention.
